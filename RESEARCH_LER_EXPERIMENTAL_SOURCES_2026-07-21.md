# Experimental LER/Roughness Data Sources for Stochastic-Roughness Validation
**Date:** 2026-07-21 · **Purpose:** identify quantitatively-rich experimental roughness datasets that can serve as calibration/validation targets for petch's stochastic-roughness layer (Krueger-style blind protocol: calibrate on some conditions, predict held-out ones). Near-term driver: partner CMOS LER etch challenge (STL files arriving ~1 week).

**Method note:** web-searched literature (titles/abstracts/some full texts verified); Krueger 2024 full PDF downloaded and grepped. Where a DOI could not be verified I give the publisher URL instead — do not cite DOIs from this file without checking.

---

## 1. Metrology fundamentals we must match (or we validate against artifacts)

The single most important lesson from this survey: **every pre-~2017 "LER = X nm" number is biased upward by SEM noise**, and PSDs below ~10 nm wavelength are dominated by white noise floor unless corrected. Any validation we do must either (a) use unbiased/PSD-corrected published data, or (b) forward-model the metrology (sample our simulated edge like a CD-SEM, add noise, run the same estimator).

Key sources:

- **C. A. Mack, "Unbiased roughness measurements: Subtracting out SEM effects," Microelectronic Engineering 191, 68–76 (2018).** [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167931718300169) · [author PDF](http://www.lithoguru.com/scientist/litho_papers/2018_Unbiased_Roughness_Measurement.pdf)
  The foundational paper: measured PSD = true PSD + white noise floor; estimate the floor from the high-frequency plateau and subtract; inverse-linescan-model (ILM) edge detection. Defines the now-standard reported quartet: **unbiased sigma (σ_LER, σ_LWR), correlation length ξ, roughness (Hurst) exponent H, and the full PSD curve** with PSD(0) as the low-frequency plateau.
- **Mack, "Unbiased roughness measurements: Subtracting out SEM effects, part 2," JVST B 36, 06J503 (2018)** [AIP](https://pubs.aip.org/avs/jvb/article-abstract/36/6/06J503/591641/) and **part 3 (SPIE Metrology 2019)** [PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/60106e4f45502509466c0e52/t/6026f87cfee4e81d3d1d639b/1613166717093/SPIE+2019+SEM+effects+part+3+Mack+(1).pdf) — robustness of noise subtraction across SEM tool settings (frames of averaging, pixel size, magnification). Part 3 shows unbiased results become tool-setting-independent — the operational definition of "unbiased."
  Full LER paper index: [lithoguru.com/scientist/papers-LER.html](https://www.lithoguru.com/scientist/papers-LER.html)
- **G. F. Lorusso et al., "Need for LWR metrology standardization: the imec roughness protocol," J. Micro/Nanolith. MEMS MOEMS (JM3) 17(4), 041009 (2018).** [SPIE open](https://www.spiedigitallibrary.org/journals/journal-of-micro-nanopatterning-materials-and-metrology/volume-17/issue-04/041009/Need-for-LWR-metrology-standardization-the-imec-roughness-protocol/10.1117/1.JMM.17.4.041009.full) — the industry protocol: measurement box ≥2 µm line length, noise correction mandatory, PSD + autocorrelation reporting. **This is the protocol our simulated-metrology layer should reproduce** (edge sampling interval, line length → frequency window ~0.5/µm to ~50/µm).
- **SEMI P47** ("Test Method for Evaluation of Line-Edge Roughness and Linewidth Roughness") and **SEMI P48** (companion CD-SEM metrology standard). [SEMI store: P47](https://store-us.semi.org/products/p04700-semi-p47-test-method-for-evaluation-of-line-edge-roughness-and-linewidth-roughness) — paywalled standards; define 3σ conventions, sampling, box length. We only need to mirror their conventions, which the Mack/Lorusso papers restate openly.
- **Constantoudis, Papavieros et al., "Line edge roughness metrology: recent challenges and advances," JM3 17(4), 041014 (2018).** [SPIE](https://www.spiedigitallibrary.org/journals/journal-of-micro-nanolithography-mems-and-moems/volume-17/issue-4/041014/) — review incl. the σ/ξ/H three-parameter self-affine model (their lineage invented it for LER), digitization effects, deep-learning denoising.
- **Naulleau & Gallatin lineage** (below, §4) established the frequency-domain transfer-function formalism we should adopt: LER transfer = multiplicative filter on the PSD.

**Who publishes FULL PSDs vs just σ:** full PSDs are routine only post-2017 and concentrated in: Mack/Fractilia papers, imec (Lorusso/De Simone) EUV resist-screening and AEI-vs-ADI papers, Pargon's CEA-Leti group (they fit PSDs to extract unbiased LWR since ~2012), and the Gogolides/Constantoudis group. Classic 2000s etch papers (Goldfarb, Oehrlein early work) report σ (biased) + sometimes correlation length; AFM-based surface papers report RMS + PSD of top surfaces. Assume any bare-σ number from before ~2015 carries +30–50% noise bias at the ~1–2 nm level.

Typical experimental magnitudes to anchor sanity checks: post-litho EUV resist unbiased 3σ LWR ≈ 3–5 nm, ξ ≈ 10–30 nm, H ≈ 0.5–0.8; post-etch 3σ typically drops 20–50% with ξ growing (etch kills high-frequency content).

## 2. Etch-induced LER experiments with process-knob dependence

### CEA-Leti / Pargon series (richest process-knob coverage, Si gate stacks, Cl2/HBr)
- **E. Pargon et al., "Linewidth roughness transfer during gate patterning" / "Benefits of plasma treatments on CD control and LWR transfer during gate patterning."** [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/258802224_Benefits_of_plasma_treatments_on_critical_dimension_control_and_line_width_roughness_transfer_during_gate_patterning) · [Academia copy](https://www.academia.edu/58045415/) — LWR measured (CD-SEM + PSD fit → unbiased) **at every step of a real gate stack etch** (resist → BARC → hard mask → poly-Si), with and without pre-etch plasma cures. Quantitative: unbiased LWR per step, PSD per step.
- **L. Azarnouche, E. Pargon et al.** (JAP 2012–2014 cluster): unbiased-LWR protocol paper (CD-SEM noise estimation via PSD fitting), **"HBr/O2 plasma treatment followed by a bake for photoresist LWR smoothing," J. Appl. Phys. 115, 074901 (2014)** [AIP](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jap/article-abstract/115/7/074901/138704/), and **"Revisiting the mechanisms involved in LWR smoothing of 193 nm photoresist patterns during HBr plasma treatment"** [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/255796963_) — knobs: treatment chemistry (HBr, H2, He, Ar, VUV-only), pulsed vs CW plasma, bake. Frequency-resolved result: **VUV/H2 synergy removes high-frequency roughness; low-frequency survives**. Also CD-AFM cross-checks ([CD-AFM study of plasma impact on 193nm PR LWR](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/253910190_)).
- **"Plasma treatments to improve line-width roughness during gate patterning"** [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/268444311_) — H2 treatment: 46% LWR reduction, dense vs iso lines.

### Oehrlein group (UMD) — fluorocarbon, best chemistry match to our SiO2/FC capability
- **"Plasma-surface interactions of advanced photoresists with C4F8/Ar discharges: Plasma parameter dependencies," JVST B 27(1), 92 (2009).** [AIP](https://pubs.aip.org/avs/jvb/article-abstract/27/1/92/467290/) — AFM surface roughness of 193/248 nm resists vs **ion energy, pressure, substrate temperature, degree of surface fluorination**; Fourier analysis of AFM maps gives a process-dependent grain-size (spatial-frequency) distribution set early (~seconds) that then **grows in amplitude only** — a strong, falsifiable prediction for a stochastic model (frozen spectrum, growing amplitude). Companion: [e-beam-generated-plasma resist roughening, JVST B 25, 779 (2007)](https://pubs.aip.org/avs/jvb/article-abstract/25/3/779/468652/). Key extra finding: **top-surface and sidewall roughness correlate**, so surface-roughening physics transfers to sidewalls. Group publication list: [lppm.umd.edu/publications](https://lppm.umd.edu/publications/).

### LPCVD-era Grenoble blanket-surface work (mechanism constraints)
- **M. Martin & G. Cunge, "Surface roughness generated by plasma etching processes of silicon," JVST B 26(4), 1281 (2008), DOI 10.1116/1.2932091.** [AIP](https://pubs.aip.org/avs/jvb/article-abstract/26/4/1281/590844/) — AFM roughness kinetics with in-situ ion and F-atom flux measurement. **Refutes naive "plasma always roughens":** high-density plasmas smooth pre-existing Si roughness; roughening requires micromasking by non-volatile redeposits from chamber walls. Any roughness model that grows roughness unconditionally is wrong on this data.
- **Nakazaki, Ono, Eriguchi et al. (Kyoto), "Two modes of surface roughening during plasma etching of silicon: role of ionized etch products."** [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/286153303_) — quantitative AFM RMS vs bias/time showing two roughening regimes; their series also gives roughness vs ion incidence angle.
- **"Kinetic etch front instability responsible for roughness formation in plasma etching," Applied Surface Science (2021).** [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169433220336217) — KMC-based instability theory beyond micromasking; useful positioning reference for what a first-principles roughening term must reproduce.

### imec EUV (resist → post-etch, modern, unbiased PSDs)
- imec 2018-onward EUV resist characterization uses full unbiased PSDs on 32 nm-pitch L/S; post-etch (AEI) vs post-litho (ADI) PSD comparisons show an **illumination-independent reduction in mid/high-frequency PSD after etch**; design rule stated explicitly: *litho owns low-frequency roughness, etch owns high-frequency (grows ξ)*. [imec magazine, Mar 2018](https://www.imec-int.com/en/imec-magazine/imec-magazine-march-2018/imec-pushes-the-limits-of-euv-lithography-single-exposure-for-future-logic-and-memory-applications) · De Simone et al., "Setting up a proper PSD and autocorrelation analysis for material and process characterization," Proc. SPIE 10585, 105851K (2018). Intel/Fractilia SPIE 2024 talk: resist material ranking **changes between ADI and AEI** — you must include etch ([lithoguru SPIE 2024 blog, day 2](https://lithoguru.com/life/?p=711)).
- **Mack, "Reducing roughness in extreme ultraviolet lithography," JM3 17(4) (2018) / SPIE 10583 (2017).** [PDF](https://www.lithoguru.com/scientist/litho_papers/2108_Reducing%20Roughness%20in%20EUVL.pdf) — PSD-parameter budget framework (σ, ξ, H, PSD(0)) incl. what etch can and cannot fix.

### Kushner-group status (direct answer to the Krueger question)
- **F. Krüger, D. Zhang, P. Luan, M. Park, A. Metz, M. J. Kushner, "Autonomous hybrid optimization of a SiO2 plasma etching mechanism," JVST A 42, 043008 (2024), DOI 10.1116/6.0003554.** [AIP](https://pubs.aip.org/avs/jva/article/42/4/043008/3300319/) · [UMich PDF](https://cpseg.eecs.umich.edu/pub/articles/JVSTA_42_043008_2024.pdf) — **Verified against full text: contains NO roughness/texture data.** The only "roughness" in the paper is the roughness of the optimizer's loss surface. Calibration/validation observables are 2D etch **profile shapes** (CD, depth, taper/bow) vs TEL experimental cross-sections, training + held-out related processes. So the Krueger blind-protocol *methodology* transfers to roughness, but the Krueger *dataset* does not contain roughness targets. Companions (Phys. Plasmas 31, 033508 (2024); JVST A 41, 013006 (2023) voltage-waveform-tailoring HAR SiO2 papers) are also profile-level, not roughness-level.
- Kushner-group roughness simulation does exist elsewhere: **X. Wang, M. Wang, P. Biolsi, M. J. Kushner, "Scaling of atomic layer etching of SiO2 in fluorocarbon plasmas: Transient etching and surface roughness," JVST A 39, 033003 (2021).** [AIP](https://pubs.aip.org/avs/jva/article/39/3/033003/1079734/) · [OSTI](https://www.osti.gov/pages/biblio/1853526) — MCFPM prediction that statistical polymer residue cycle-to-cycle produces spatially varying EPC → ALE roughness; simulation-only (no experimental PSD).
- Earlier MCFPM-3D work simulated **contact edge roughness / bowing in SiO2 etch** (AVS 2014 program, [PDF](https://www2.avs.org/symposium2014/ProgramBooks/ProgramBook_Session_PS2-WeM.pdf)); Huard et al., "Transient behavior in quasi-ALE of SiO2/Si3N4 in fluorocarbon plasmas," JVST A 36, 06B101 (2018) [AIP](https://pubs.aip.org/avs/jva/article-abstract/36/6/06B101/246166/).

### Misc quantitative sets

## 3. Sidewall roughness (vs top-down LER) — who has real data

Hard-won consensus: sidewall PSDs are rare; most groups infer sidewall behavior from top-down LER + occasional AFM heroics.

- **D. L. Goldfarb et al. (IBM + Sawin/MIT), "Effect of thin-film imaging on line edge roughness transfer to underlayers during etch processes," JVST B 22(2), 647–653 (2004).** [AIP](https://pubs.aip.org/avs/jvb/article-abstract/22/2/647/470299/) — the classic: **conventional AFM tipped into trenches to map 3D sidewall roughness** of resist and etched underlayers. Findings: BARC-open + substrate etch can *reduce* sidewall roughness vs developed resist; more "physical" (ion-dominated) etches imprint **vertical striations** on both resist and substrate sidewalls. Quantitative sidewall RMS maps; pre-PSD-era statistics.
- **Gogolides-group tilted/sidewall analyses:** "Effects of resist sidewall morphology on LER reduction and transfer during etching: is the resist sidewall after development isotropic or anisotropic?" [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/236585915_) — measured **anisotropic sidewall correlation structure** (vertical vs horizontal), directly relevant to how we should seed sidewall noise in 3D.
- **3D-AFM (tilted-Z-scanner, Park Systems): "Automated measurement and analysis of sidewall roughness using three-dimensional atomic force microscopy," Applied Microscopy 52, 8 (2022), DOI 10.1186/s42649-022-00070-5.** [open access](https://appmicro.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s42649-022-00070-5) — Z-scanner tilts ±38°; **sidewall PSD, height-height correlation, autocorrelation reported; <2% RSD reproducibility**. The best modern demonstration that sidewall PSDs are measurable; limited condition coverage (metrology paper, not process study).
- **Tilting-AFM vs SEM reference metrology: "Direct comparison of LER measurements by SEM and a metrological tilting-AFM," JM3 19(4), 044001 (2020).** [SPIE](https://www.spiedigitallibrary.org/journals/journal-of-micro-nanolithography-mems-and-moems/volume-19/issue-04/044001/) — cross-calibrates top-down SEM LER against true 3D edge.
- **AFM-based metrology for advanced etch in 3D ICs (review, Micromachines 17, 565 (2026-05)).** [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-666X/17/5/565) — current-state survey of sidewall metrology incl. FinFET fins ([Si-fin sidewall roughness by AFM](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/224407565_)).
- **Photonics community = sleeper dataset for sidewall PSDs of etched Si/SiN:** waveguide-loss people measure exactly σ, ξ, H of **etched sidewalls**. "Measurements and modeling of atomic-scale sidewall roughness and losses in integrated photonic devices" [arXiv 2105.11477](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.11477) (rotated-axis AFM, ~0.1 nm sensitivity); "Wafer-scale waveguide sidewall roughness scattering loss characterization by image processing" (2025) [arXiv 2501.11590](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.11590) · [OSTI](https://www.osti.gov/pages/biblio/2565044) — top-down-SEM-derived sidewall PSDs across 24 devices, validated against measured optical loss (Payne–Lacey model); SOI ridge waveguide loss/SWR/width correlation [IEEE](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/4895221/). These give absolute sidewall σ (~0.5–3 nm), ξ (~30–100 nm) for fluorine-etched Si/SiN — directly comparable to our engine's output, with an independent physical cross-check (loss).

## 4. Roughness TRANSFER studies (input PSD + output PSD both measured — cleanest validation)

- **Formalism: P. Naulleau & G. Gallatin (LBNL/NIST):** "Line-edge roughness transfer function and its application to determining mask effects in EUV resist characterization," Appl. Opt. 42(17) (2003) [PDF](https://goldberg.lbl.gov/papers/Naulleau_AO_42(17)2003.pdf); "Effect of resist on the transfer of LER spatial metrics from mask to wafer," JVST B 28(6), 1259 (2010) [AIP](https://pubs.aip.org/avs/jvb/article-abstract/28/6/1259/582247/) · [OSTI](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/961833); NIST companion on mask→wafer LER transfer modeling [NIST](https://www.nist.gov/publications/modeling-transfer-line-edge-roughness-euv-mask-wafer). Defines the **frequency-dependent LER transfer function (LTF)** — mask→resist, but the identical formalism applies to resist→etched-layer; adopt PSD_out(f) = |T(f)|² PSD_in(f) + PSD_intrinsic(f).
- **Gogolides/Constantoudis/Kokkoris + Pargon/Martin (joint modeling+experiment — the closest existing template for what petch wants to do):**
  - **V. Constantoudis, G. Kokkoris, P. Xydi, E. Gogolides, E. Pargon, M. Martin, "Line-edge-roughness transfer during plasma etching: modeling approaches and comparison with experimental results," JM3 8(4), 043004 (2009), DOI 10.1117/1.3268365.** [SPIE](https://www.spiedigitallibrary.org/journals/journal-of-micro-nanolithography-mems-and-moems/volume-8/issue-4/043004/) · [HAL](https://dumas.ccsd.cnrs.fr/FMNT/hal-00399796v1) — 3D ion-transfer model + 2D phenomenological isotropic-front model vs **measured σ/ξ/H before/after**: trim-only, transfer-only, trim+transfer scenarios on resist/underlayer stacks. Both models predict σ reduction on transfer; experiments confirm.
  - **G. Kokkoris et al., "Modeling of line edge roughness transfer during plasma etching," Microelectronic Engineering 86 (2009).** [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167931709000641)
  - **"LER transfer during plasma etching: modeling approaches and comparison with experimental results" (extended)** [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/236585916_)
- **Frequency-resolved transfer measurements:**
  - **"Line edge roughness frequency analysis during pattern transfer in semiconductor fabrication," JM3 14(3), 033501 (2015).** [ADS](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015JMM%26M..14c3501S/abstract) · [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/281801264_) — PSD split into low/mid/high bands through a full SADP flow; finding: **components with wavelength <200 nm are not fully transferred at each plasma step** (per-step LWR decrease); detects wiggling quantitatively. Companion: "Spectral analysis of LW/LER transfer during self-aligned double patterning" [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/282859065_) and "LER frequency analysis for SAQP," Proc. SPIE 9780 (2016) [ADS](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2016SPIE.9780E..1SS/abstract).
  - CEA-Leti gate-stack per-step PSDs (§2, Pargon series) are effectively measured transfer functions through 3–4 sequential etch steps.
  - imec ADI→AEI PSD pairs (§2) are single-step transfer functions on EUV stacks (Si3N4/SiN hard masks, CH2F2/N2/Ar chemistries; e.g., the Si3N4/EUV-resist CH2F2/N2/Ar LER study in [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167931712001700)).

**Consensus transfer phenomenology every model must hit:** (i) etch attenuates high-frequency LER (T(f) < 1 above a cutoff set by ~etch-front lateral correlation), (ii) low-frequency LER passes ~unattenuated (T→1 as f→0), (iii) ξ grows through etch, (iv) ion-dominated steps can *add* intrinsic high-frequency sidewall texture (striations), (v) total σ usually decreases resist→substrate unless micromasking/redeposition kicks in.

## 5. Modeling prior art to position against

- **Gogolides/Constantoudis/Kokkoris (NCSR Demokritos)** — the measure-AND-model lineage. Stochastic/level-set-hybrid + Monte Carlo LER-transfer simulators, validated against their own CD-SEM/AFM σ/ξ/H data (§4). Also: "Towards control of plasma-induced surface roughness: simultaneous etching+deposition" [EPJ AP](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/the-european-physical-journal-applied-physics/article/abs/23C4F406D864BE0B2C8820A8E9BAF8F5) and polymer-substrate roughness+charging KMC with ion reflection/SEE [Micromachines 9, 415 (2018)](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-666X/9/8/415). Validation level: parameter trends + PSD shapes, not blind prediction.
- **Sawin group (MIT), Guo & Wang:** 3D cellular Monte Carlo with **mixing-layer kinetics**: "Mixing-layer kinetics model for plasma etching and the cellular realization in a 3D profile simulator," JVST A 27(2), 388 (2009) [AIP](https://pubs.aip.org/avs/jva/article-abstract/27/2/388/381616/); "Etching of SiO2 in C4F8/Ar plasmas II: simulation of surface roughening and local polymerization," JVST A 28(2), 259 (2010) [AIP](https://pubs.aip.org/avs/jva/article-abstract/28/2/259/244414/). **Best validation of the lot:** simulated planar roughening vs ion incidence angle (0–82°, flux ratios 5 and 20) compared against their own reactive-ion-beam + AFM experiments — smooth ≤45°, perpendicular striations at 60–75°, perpendicular→parallel ripple transition reproduced. Review of the whole subfield: **"Review of profile and roughening simulation in microelectronics plasma etching," J. Phys. D 42, 194014 (2009)** [IOP](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/0022-3727/42/19/194014).
- **Kushner group (MCFPM/MCFPM-3D):** voxel Monte Carlo, inherently stochastic; simulated contact-edge roughness, ALE roughness from statistical polymer residue (§2). Validation is profile-level (CDs, taper, ARDE) against industrial cross-sections; **no published PSD-level roughness validation found** — that gap is exactly petch's opening.
- **Resist-side stochastic simulators** (Mack analytic PSD models; Demokritos molecular-resist KMC [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/236592481_); "LER from EUV lithography to FinFET: computational study" [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8706712/)) — provide the *input* edge statistics for an etch-transfer simulation.
- **Positioning summary:** nobody currently offers a fast/differentiable engine that takes a measured resist PSD in and predicts the post-etch PSD blind. Sawin validated angle physics on blanket surfaces (2010, dead lineage); Demokritos validated trends; Kushner has the machinery but validates profiles, not spectra. A Krueger-style blind PSD-transfer validation would be a first.

## 6. Verdict — ranked validation targets

**Confirmed: top-down LER/LWR PSD transfer through etch is the right first observable.** It is the only roughness observable where input AND output are both measured with modern unbiased protocols, published as full PSD curves, across process knobs. Sidewall PSDs are too sparse to calibrate on (use them as spot checks); blanket-surface AFM roughening is a good secondary because it matches our fluorocarbon/SiO2 chemistry better than the gate-stack LER literature.

Ranking (data richness × condition coverage × system match × blind-protocol fitness):

1. **CEA-Leti/Pargon gate-patterning series (2011–2015)** — unbiased PSDs at *every* stack step, across ≥5 pre-treatments and multiple etch chemistries (HBr/Cl2/O2, Si/poly-Si). Blind protocol: calibrate the per-step transfer function on a subset of treatments/steps, predict held-out ones. Weakness: Si/Cl2-HBr chemistry (matches our Si capability, not SiO2/FC); data must be digitized from figures.
2. **Demokritos + Leti joint set (Constantoudis/Kokkoris/Gogolides + Pargon/Martin, JM3 8, 043004 (2009))** — purpose-built model-vs-experiment σ/ξ/H before/after in three scenarios (trim, transfer, trim+transfer). Smaller, but explicitly designed for exactly our comparison; ideal first reproduction target before attempting blind prediction on (1).
3. **imec ADI→AEI EUV PSD pairs (2018–2024, incl. Intel/Fractilia AEI ranking studies)** — most modern and metrology-clean; directly matches the partner's CMOS-LER challenge framing (EUV resist → etch). Weakness: proprietary scatter across SPIE papers; stacks are multi-layer (need our multi-material 3D engine, feature_step_3d).
4. **Oehrlein C4F8/Ar resist-roughening set (JVST B 27, 92 (2009) + companions)** — AFM PSDs vs ion energy/pressure/temperature in *our* fluorocarbon chemistry; the "frozen spectrum, growing amplitude" observation is a sharp falsifiable test for the noise-injection statistics themselves (calibrate at one condition, predict spectrum evolution at others). Blanket surfaces = no feature transport confound.
5. **Guo & Sawin ion-beam angle-resolved roughening/ripple set (JVST A 2010)** — clean single-knob (angle) physics validation of angle-dependent stochastic yield + curvature terms; plus **photonics sidewall PSDs** (arXiv 2105.11477, 2501.11590) as absolute-sidewall-σ/ξ spot checks for the partner challenge geometry.

**Practical sequencing for petch:** (a) implement metrology-twin first (sample simulated edges per imec protocol, PSD estimator with noise floor + Mack subtraction) — without it no comparison is meaningful; (b) reproduce target 2 (σ/ξ/H before/after, 3 scenarios); (c) blind-protocol on target 1 (fit T(f) parameters on subset, predict held-out); (d) use targets 4/5 to constrain the noise-injection term independently of transfer; (e) photonics sidewall PSDs + Goldfarb striation phenomenology for the partner STL challenge (report sidewall σ/ξ/H on the delivered geometry).

**Krueger question answered definitively:** JVST A 42, 043008 (2024) and its waveform-tailoring companions contain **no roughness data** (verified full-text); their blind calibrate/hold-out methodology is the part to reuse, pointed at the PSD-transfer datasets above.
