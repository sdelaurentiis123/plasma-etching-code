## Use in petch

The paper is adjacent same-group device evidence. Its dimensions are recorded in
the Zhu manifest so a later SEM can be classified against a published relevant
feature scale, but they are not injected into the exact-condition geometry.
The strongest eventual end-to-end gate is not merely matching an etch depth: it
is predicting the distribution of final pillar widths, sidewall angles, missing
or collapsed small pillars, and their optical consequence across a metasurface
layout.
