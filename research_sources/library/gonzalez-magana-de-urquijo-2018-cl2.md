## Claims table

| # | verified source claim | use and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | A pulsed-Townsend apparatus measured electron drift velocity, density-normalized longitudinal diffusion, and effective ionization in Cl2 and Cl2/N2. The pure-Cl2 measurements used `1.1--6.1 Torr` and `293--298 K`. | This is the later direct transport board requested by the 2012 compilation. It constrains collision physics, not a low-pressure reactor state. |
| Q2 | The authors compare their drift and effective-ionization measurements with the only two earlier measurements, one of which was indirect, and use Cl2/N2 mixtures to test completeness and reliability of the Cl2 cross-section set. | A predictive cross-section deck must be graded against these measurements rather than only the older swarm data used to construct it. |
| Q3 | Typical reported measurement uncertainties are +/-2% for drift velocity, +/-5--9% for effective ionization, and +/-10--15% for density-normalized longitudinal diffusion. | The executable board carries these as source-wide intervals; it does not invent point-specific sigma values. |
| Q4 | Tables A1--A3 print 23 pure-Cl2 drift markers, 21 effective-ionization markers, and eight longitudinal-diffusion markers. The body states a pure-Cl2 range of `100--420 Td`, while Table A1 prints drift values through `460 Td`. | All 52 printed markers are preserved and the body/table conflict remains explicit. |
| Q5 | The new critical reduced field is `280 Td`, 4.6% below the older reported `293 Td`. The direct drift trend differs by as much as about 12% from the earlier indirect curve in their overlap. | Confirms that the legacy swarm anchor is not precise enough to certify a modern transport deck by itself. |
