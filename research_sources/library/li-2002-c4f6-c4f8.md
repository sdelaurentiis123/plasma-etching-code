## Claims table

| # | verified source claim | use and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | The matched ICP used 600 W inductive power, 6 or 20 mTorr, 40 sccm total flow, a 10 °C wafer holder, and independently controlled 3.7 MHz bias through -100 V. | Defines a quantitative reactor-model validation board. It is not Krüger's high-power dual-frequency CCP and cannot normalize that trench. |
| Q2 | Diagnostics jointly measured wafer-center ion saturation current, absolute CF/CF2/COF2 densities, real-time deposition/etch rates and film thickness, and post-process XPS composition. | This is unusually strong multi-observable conditioning: a reactor/surface model must match flux proxies, surface state, and etch response together rather than one depth. |
| Q3 | In C4F6, CF2 was about 50 times CF at 20 mTorr; both fell with Ar addition, and CF2 remained the dominant measured radical. | A single generic fluorocarbon-neutral channel is not a fundamental boundary. Figure values must be digitized before any numerical rate is landed. |
| Q4 | C4F6 deposited fluorocarbon faster, etched that film slower, and etched SiO2 more slowly than C4F8 under otherwise matched conditions. | Directly forbids treating C4F6 as a small parameter delta from a C4F8 surface deck. |
| Q5 | The steady-state C4F6-derived film was generally thicker and less fluorinated; thicker films coincided with lower Si and SiO2 yields, but C4F8 still gave higher yield at a matched film thickness. | Film thickness is necessary but not sufficient state. Film composition and feed-specific ion/radical chemistry must remain explicit. |
| Q6 | At high C4F6-rich source power the chamber pressure collapsed because of rapid dissociation; this did not occur for Ar-rich mixtures. | Feed fraction, dissociation, pressure control, and residence/wall loss are coupled. A fixed-pressure zero-dimensional model must reproduce the operating-domain failure rather than extrapolate through it. |

etch rates, absolute material yields, film thickness, and XPS-derived F/C
