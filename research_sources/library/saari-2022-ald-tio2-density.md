# saari-2022-ald-tio2-density

**Saari et al., ALD TiO2 density and amorphous-to-rutile crystallization**

- **Citation:** J. Saari et al., “Low-Temperature Route to Direct Amorphous
  to Rutile Crystallization of TiO2 Thin Films Grown by Atomic Layer
  Deposition,” *J. Phys. Chem. C* (2022).
- **DOI:** `10.1021/acs.jpcc.2c04905`
- **Primary author manuscript:**
  `https://trepo.tuni.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/215966/acs.jpcc.2c04905_1.pdf?sequence=1`
- **Status:** PRIMARY FULL TEXT READ ONLINE; PDF NOT LOCALLY ARCHIVED
- **Topic:** ALD TiO2 XRR density, phase, defects, and growth-temperature
  dependence

## Claims table

| # | verified source claim | use and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Table 1 reports XRR densities of `3.52` and `3.94 g cm-3` for as-deposited TDMAT/H2O ALD TiO2 grown at `100` and `200 C`, respectively. | Direct evidence that ALD process history materially changes the formula-unit density used in depth conversion. |
| Q2 | The paper explicitly reports the Go et al. oxygen-plasma-duration split: `3.73 g cm-3` amorphous TiO2 for a `3 s` exposure and `4.15 g cm-3` anatase TiO2 for a `30 s` exposure. | Upper endpoint of a broad material sensitivity, not a target coefficient. This row is a primary-paper relay to Go et al.; it must not be represented as Saari's own Table-1 measurement. |
| Q3 | The source attributes the crystallization path to growth-temperature-dependent defects and impurities. | Supports the requirement to obtain Zhu's ALD route/phase before tightening the density interval. |

## Use in petch

The `4.15 g cm-3` literature-envelope endpoint is used only to expose density
sensitivity in the target-free Zhu atom/dose gate.  It is not a probability
bound and is superseded by a measurement of the supplied film.
