## Use in petch

`CHOI-2013-TIO2-BIAS-RESPONSE-R1` exactly records the source-reported
endpoints. A two-point square-root-of-bias decomposition is retained only as
an algebraic diagnostic and explicitly rejected as an identified surface law.
Together with the Ji same-gas morphology boards, this source fixes the minimum
future TiO2 topology: fluorination, neutral supply, ion-energy-dependent
desorption, and competitive passivation/blocking. It does not change the
absolute Oxford prediction.
