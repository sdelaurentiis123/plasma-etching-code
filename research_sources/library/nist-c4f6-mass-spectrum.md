# nist-c4f6-mass-spectrum

**NIST Chemistry WebBook electron-ionization spectrum of 1,3-C4F6**

- **Primary record:** NIST Chemistry WebBook SRD 69, CAS `685-63-2`, NIST
  Mass Spectrum `5987`.
- **URL:** `https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?Index=0&Large=on&Spec=C685632&Type=Mass`
- **Status:** PRIMARY DATABASE IMAGE + ORIGINAL-RESOLUTION PIL AUDIT; SOURCE
  PIXELS NOT REDISTRIBUTED.
- **Consumed data:** 23 checksum-bound stick tops in
  `data/experimental/nist_c4f6_mass_spectrum/`.
- **Topic:** measured direct electron-ionization fragmentation topology for
  the C4F6 parent molecule.

## Claims table

| # | verified source claim | use and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | The 800 x 600 printable spectrum has `C3F3+` as the base peak and intact `C4F6+` at 43.86 relative-intensity units. | Direct parent ionization retains heavy products. A C4F6 collision row cannot be mapped only to light `CFx+` fragments. |
| Q2 | Direct `CF+`, `CF2+`, and `CF3+` sticks are 15.23, 1.59, and 4.77, giving `CF2+/CF+ = 0.1045` and `CF3+/CF+ = 0.3134`. | A source-level fragmentation prior and topology check, not an EEDF-integrated partial cross section or plasma flux. |
| Q3 | `C3F3+` plus intact `C4F6+` carry 70.3% of the monoisotopic stick intensity. | Secondary fragmentation/ionization, ion-neutral chemistry, and mass-resolved wall transport must remain explicit when explaining Benck's light-ion currents. |

## Rights and use boundary

The WebBook states that this mass spectrum cannot be downloaded. The source
image and visual overlay remain local and are represented only by their hash,
dimensions, pixel calibration, and the derived numerical audit. Relative EI
intensity is neither an absolute cross section nor an absolute plasma ion
flux. It cannot identify Krueger's species mixture or normalize a feature
depth.
