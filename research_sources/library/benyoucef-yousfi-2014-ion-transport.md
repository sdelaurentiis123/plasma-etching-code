# benyoucef-yousfi-2014-ion-transport

**Benyoucef & Yousfi, calculated and transport-validated parent-ion cross sections**

- **Citation:** D. Benyoucef and M. Yousfi, “Ar+/Ar, O2+/O2 and N2+/N2
  Elastic Momentum Collision Cross Sections: Calculation and Validation Using
  the Semi-Classical Model,” *Plasma Science and Technology* 16, 588–592
  (2014).
- **DOI:** `10.1088/1009-0630/16/6/09`
- **Primary record:** `https://doi.org/10.1088/1009-0630/16/6/09`
- **Status:** AUTHOR-PROVIDED FULL TEXT READ VIA HTML; FIGURES NOT LOCALLY
  ARCHIVED
- **Topic:** independent Ar+/Ar, O2+/O2, N2+/N2 transport validation

## Claims table

| # | verified source claim | use and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Momentum-transfer cross sections are calculated from a semiclassical JWKB phase-shift treatment using a 12-6-4 interparticle potential. | Independent physics route for checking the Phelps-based argon closure. |
| Q2 | The paper computes ion mobility and drift velocity with Monte Carlo transport and compares them with published pulsed-Townsend measurements. | Defines the right intermediate observables for a future transport-only validation gate. |
| Q3 | Reported calculated-versus-measured transport deviations are below 3% for the three parent-ion systems. | Corroboration only until the curves are locally digitized and independently regraded. |
| Q4 | The charge-exchange and elastic momentum-transfer curves are separate, and the collision energy is a relative collision energy. | Guards energy-frame and process-definition mistakes. |

## Use decision

This paper is not used to select a reactor density or etch depth. It is an
independent validation route for ion transport and a natural cross-chemistry
extension after argon. Figure pixels could not be archived through the
publisher endpoint in this session, so the reported 3% result is not yet a
project-reproduced grade.
