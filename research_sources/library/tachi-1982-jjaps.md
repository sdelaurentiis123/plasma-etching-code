## Import firewall

This paper is not a numerical authority in petch. Its accessible abstract
establishes that relevant mass-selected high-energy fluorocarbon experiments
exist, but not their digitizable values, uncertainty, angle, or SiO2 transfer.
A full-text, original-figure audit is required before any number can enter a
surface deck. Even then, elemental-Si results would remain a cross-target
mechanism constraint rather than a SiO2 yield closure.
