# woodworth-1999-commercial-vuv

**Absolute VUV at a wafer in a commercial chlorine metal etcher**

- **Citation:** J. R. Woodworth, M. G. Blain, R. L. Jarecki, T. W.
  Hamilton, and B. P. Aragon, “Absolute intensities of the vacuum ultraviolet
  spectra in a metal-etch plasma processing discharge,” *JVST A* **17**,
  3209--3217 (1999).
- **DOI:** `10.1116/1.582044`
- **Status:** PRIMARY PUBLISHER RECORD/ABSTRACT; FULL PDF NOT ARCHIVED

## Claims table

| # | verified source claim | use and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | In a commercial ICP with a standard Cl2/BCl3 aluminum-etch mixture, measured wafer photon flux above 8.8 eV is about 4e14 cm^-2 s^-1 for a nonreactive wafer and 0.7e14 for a reactive wafer. | External amplitude comparator only until full methods/uncertainty are archived. |
| W2 | Nearly all light above 8.8 eV is atomic Cl, mostly 8.9--9.3 eV; optical trapping complicates calculation. | Supports strong 133--139 nm emission and rejects transparent-volume scaling as prediction. |

## Use decision

These abstract numbers may bound plausibility in an identifiability report but
cannot become a calibrated production constant until the full article is
retrieved and audited.
