## Claims table

| # | verified source claim | use and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Light/ion blocking experiments show conclusively that substantial below-threshold p-Si etching is caused by photons, predominantly wavelengths below 170 nm, not Cl-only chemistry, Ar metastables, or low-energy ion neutralization. | Establishes PAE as a required chlorine/Si channel; it does not provide an absolute photon flux. |
| S2 | IAE begins at about 16 eV and scales with square-root energy; at 36 eV the PAE and IAE rates are equal in 1% Cl2/Ar. | PAE is not a tiny correction near low-energy processing conditions. |
| S3 | The relative subthreshold component is even larger in pure Cl2, but the paper does not publish a pure-Cl2 absolute photon spectrum or yield. | Supports mechanism presence in Mahorowala chemistry but cannot normalize it. |
| S4 | The studied material is p-type Si and the authors discuss carrier-mediated paths. | Poly-Si doping/conductivity becomes a required transfer variable when PAE is invoked. |

## Use decision

PAE may be added to the mechanism inventory, but no Mahorowala depth can select
its normalization. Absolute spectrum and RF-conditioned surface response remain
independent boundary measurements.
