# griffin-1995-cl

**Calculated elastic and inelastic electron scattering from atomic chlorine**

- **Citation:** D. C. Griffin, M. S. Pindzola, T. W. Gorczyca, and N. R.
  Badnell, “Elastic and inelastic scattering of electrons from Ar and Cl,”
  *Physical Review A* **51**, 2265--2276 (1995).
- **DOI:** `10.1103/PhysRevA.51.2265`
- **Status:** PRIMARY APS RECORD AND ABSTRACT READ; FULL ARTICLE REQUIRED BEFORE
  NUMERIC USE
- **Topic:** atomic-chlorine elastic, excitation, and ionization calculations

## Claims table

| # | verified source claim | use and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Elastic and excitation cross sections for neutral Ar and Cl are calculated with an R-matrix method including dipole-polarization effects through polarization pseudostates; only metastable excitation is included. | The atomic-Cl elastic table has calculation-grade provenance and a restricted excitation scope. |
| Q2 | Argon calculations are compared with calculations and measurements to assess the reliability of the analogous chlorine calculation. | Agreement for Ar is indirect validation of Cl, not an atomic-Cl measurement. |
| Q3 | Ionization is calculated using a distorted-wave approximation and final-state correlation is reported as important. | The paper is relevant to a source-replay audit, but newer evaluated atomic-Cl ionization evidence remains the predictive authority already used in the code. |

## Executable decision

No numeric curve is imported from the abstract. The full article is required
to map the official COMSOL atomic-Cl momentum-transfer nodes and to audit the
published energy support and angular integration.
