## Use decision

Use these measurements to grade radiative states, not electron-impact
excitation. They expose a real limitation in the initial hybrid: some of the
most relevant 108--110 nm states cannot be joined from the old compact NIST
ADF04 level record without the modern Oliver--Hibbert term table.
