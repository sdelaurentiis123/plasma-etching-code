VERIFIED EXTRACT — thesis pp. 116-119 (PDF pp. 139-143)

"Precise FC film deposition is the source of chemical etchant during the
etch step."

Metzler reports that thicker deposited FC films supply more F but also inhibit
energy deposition into the substrate.  For the 40 s ion step, Si and SiO2
removal increases with deposited film thickness and then saturates because
more of the ion step is spent removing the film.

At nominal 30 eV, FC removal is faster than at 25 eV.  SiO2 removal approaches
a reactant-limited saturation with ion-step duration.  Si instead shows
roughly constant or slightly lower removal at longer ion steps.  The thesis
attributes the Si behavior to F depletion plus a fluorinated/oxidized reacted
surface layer; trace oxygen can originate from erosion of the quartz coupling
window.

VERIFIED EXTRACT — thesis pp. 130-136 (PDF pp. 153-159)

The film F/C ratio is obtained from decomposed C 1s spectra.  The source
defines delta-F/C as F 1s/C 1s minus the film F/C ratio and uses it as an
indicator of substrate fluorination.  It is a relative XPS proxy, not an
absolute areal inventory.

For a nominal 5 Å film, the film F/C ratio falls rapidly during 25 eV ion
bombardment while delta-F/C rises.  Metzler states:

"Therefore, F is preferentially removed from the FC film and mixing with the
Si substrate takes place."

For a nominal 11 Å film, F/C decreases more slowly and delta-F/C remains close
to zero through the cycle.  The thesis says the substrate is then in a fairly
steady state while the film and substrate are removed together.

SIMS on the 5 Å, 25 eV, 40 s condition is reported to agree with the XPS
interpretation.  The measured near-surface C/F/O/Si mixed layer extends to
approximately 15 Å.  F and O are concentrated at the surface and decrease to
approximately 25 Å depth.

CLAIM BOUNDARY
- The plotted "FC thickness" is ellipsometric optical thickness.
- The source does not supply the density/composition trajectory required to
  turn every optical Å into separate C and F areal atom inventories.
- delta-F/C is not an absolute substrate-F inventory.
- The source does not publish a species-resolved wafer ion/neutral flux deck
  or an ion energy-angle distribution.
- Figures 6.5, 6.6, 6.14, and 6.15 are replayed in
  data/experimental/metzler_2016/.
