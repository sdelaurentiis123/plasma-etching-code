VERIFIED CLAIMS
- A quartz-crystal microbalance directly measures SiO2 etch/deposition yield
  versus ion energy, ion-to-neutral ratio, and incidence angle.
- At 500 V bias, Ar sputtering approaches 0.25 SiO2 formula units per ion,
  while the C2HF5 fluorocarbon-plasma yield is about 2 per ion.  The authors
  attribute the factor above Ar+/F beam data to reactive CFx+ ions; CF+ is the
  dominant positive ion for most reported C2HF5 conditions.
- Neutral-only deposition is approximately 30% of total deposition.  The
  remaining ion-associated deposition is approximately two to three times the
  ion flux under the reported convention.
- Film mass is converted using an assumed PTFE density of 2.1 g cm^-3.
- The published active-site model balances ion-created sites with neutral
  sticking, then combines neutral deposition and ion-driven removal.
- Its fitted ion site-creation yield is large and sensitive to assumed
  monomer mass and to omitted ion-energy, flux-ratio, and FC-removal effects.
  It is therefore a topology clue, not an importable atomic constant.

CLAIM BOUNDARY
The plasma-integrated yield includes the measured ion mixture and surface
synergy.  It must not be called a mass-selected CF+ yield or copied into a
different ion mixture.  The fitted active-site parameters are not direct
measurements.
