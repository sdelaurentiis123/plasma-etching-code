VERIFIED TEXT ANCHORS (short excerpts followed by exact location/meaning)

1. "The NNP is trained to learn only the DFT data with the ZBL potential
subtracted" — section 2.1.3, equation 1 discussion. The collision potential is
E_NNP = E_DFT - E_ZBL.

2. "Each ion is injected at normal incidence with a fixed kinetic energy." —
section 2.3.1. The Figure 3 simulations therefore do not provide an angular
response law.

3. "We assume that a sufficiently long time interval exists between ion
impacts" — section 2.3.2. Predefined product clusters are removed after each
impact; any cluster more than 1 nm above the slab is also removed.

4. "etch yield, which is defined as the number of Si atoms removed per ion
incidence" — section 3.1.2. Figure 3 uses 5e16 cm^-2, reported as 4500 impacts
over a 3 nm by 3 nm cell.

5. "a slight overestimation of etch yield at low energies" — section 3.1.2.
The paper identifies CF+ below 500 eV and delayed deposition onset.

6. "the thickness of the etched film before entering the deposition regime
exceeds 10 nm" — section 3.1.3. The experimental value quoted by the authors is
2 nm.

7. "restricting byproduct removal to within 2 nm (an arbitrary value)" —
section 3.1.3. The restriction improves agreement; the authors retain
all-depth removal rather than fit a removal height per ion/energy.

8. "does not explicitly include radicals or neutrals" — conclusion. The
released calculations isolate ion-induced behavior and do not close a
plasma-reactor boundary.

9. "The codes used for simulation and data analysis are available" — data
availability statement, followed by https://github.com/HyungminAn/etchZBL .
