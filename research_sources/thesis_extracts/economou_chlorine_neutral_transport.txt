Visual verification
-------------------
Both author-hosted primary PDFs were read. The 1995 scan was rendered at
500 dpi; printed pages 576–577 were checked for the chemistry, boundary
conditions, reduced diffusivity, and 500 K base condition. The 2002 article
was rendered at 400 dpi; printed page 470 was checked for Eq. 10, Tables II–III,
the 300 K base condition, wall boundary, residence time, and reduced
diffusivity.

1995 model
----------
The neutral module solves diffusion/reaction separately from the charged
particle module. For Cl at the wall, the boundary ledger contains a thermal
neutral loss proportional to the surface reaction probability and a return
term from neutralized Cl+ ions. The wafer and non-wafer walls use different
reaction probabilities.

The paper states that neutral diffusivities were obtained from standard
Chapman–Enskog kinetic theory using Lennard–Jones parameters from its ref. 12.
The actual parameters and collision integral are not tabulated. The printed
chlorine reduced diffusivity is

  N D_Cl = 6.21e18 cm^-1 s^-1
         = 6.21e20 m^-1 s^-1.

The base condition on printed page 577 is 10 mTorr, 3560 W plasma power,
13.56 MHz, 200 sccm pure chlorine, and gas temperature 500 K.

2002 model
----------
The Cl atom equation is diffusion dominated with volumetric reaction sources
and a convective loss n_Cl/tau_res. The printed residence time is 3.8 ms.
The wall boundary is

  -D_Cl grad(n_Cl)
    = [gamma/(2(2-gamma))] n_Cl vbar_Cl - J_Cl+

with gamma = 0.1 in the model. The Cl+ term is the atom return associated with
ion neutralization and is not part of neutral recombination loss.

Table II specifies 20 mTorr, 320 W peak power, 10 kHz pulsing, duty ratio 0.5,
and gas/ion temperatures of 300 K. Table III prints

  N D_Cl = 6.21e18 cm^-1 s^-1

and cites the 1995 paper.

Conflict and executable boundary
--------------------------------
The identical coefficient appears at 500 K in 1995 and at 300 K in 2002 even
though the original method is Chapman–Enskog transport. The 2002 table does not
provide a new measured or calculated temperature dependence; it cites the
1995 source.

The repository therefore keeps two exact, single-temperature
`published_model` providers for reproducing the papers. Neither supports
prediction, and neither may be extrapolated between 300 and 500 K. Predictive
closure requires measured/evaluated Cl–Cl2 transport versus temperature with
uncertainty.
