ALD                                                       has been widely characterized for its physical and optical properties, but systematic information about the in­

TiO2

hardness                                                  stress, elastic modulus and hardness of as-grown ALD TiO2 thin films on silicon were studied at temperature
                                                          range from 80 to 350 ◦ C and influence of post-ALD thermal annealing was studied on films annealed up to 900
                                                          ◦
                                                            C. ALD TiO2 films were under tensile stress in the scale of hundreds of MPa. The stress depended both on the
                                                          ALD temperature and film thickness in a complex way, and onset of crystallization increased the residual stress.
                                                          Films grown at 110 and 300 ◦ C were able to withstand post-ALD annealing at 420 ◦ C without major change in
                                                          residual stress, refractive index or extinction coefficient. Elastic modulus and hardness increased upon crystal­
                                                          lization with increasing ALD temperature. The results presented here help to improve the design of the optical
                                                          devices by choosing films with desired optical properties, and further help to design the post-ALD thermal budget
                                                          so that films maintain their desired features.
