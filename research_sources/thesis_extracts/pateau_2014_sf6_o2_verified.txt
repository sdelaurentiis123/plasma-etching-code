Observed/model validation trend:
O2 titrates SFx into SOxFy and releases F; F density rises with O2 to a
mid-mixture maximum before SF6 starvation. This topology matters even for a
small O2 recipe fraction, but the target magnitude must emerge from the Zhu
reactor state rather than transfer from Pateau's ICP.
