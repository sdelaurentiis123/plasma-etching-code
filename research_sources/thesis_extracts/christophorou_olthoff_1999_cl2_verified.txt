Verified from journal pages 131, 138--139, 148--150, 160--161:

- The review supplies suggested Cl2 cross sections for total/elastic
  scattering, total ionization, neutral dissociation, attachment, and
  ion-pair formation.
- It identifies missing measurements for Cl2 momentum transfer, vibrational
  excitation, electronic excitation, and dissociative ionization.
- Atomic-Cl excitation channels shown are calculated 4s, 5s, 6s, 4p, 5p,
  3d, 4d, and 5d states.
- Hayes et al. measured atomic-Cl single ionization from threshold to 200 eV
  with a quoted absolute uncertainty of +/-14%.
- Table 25 gives 48 selected Hayes energy/cross-section pairs in eV and
  10^-20 m2. The full visual transcription is in research_sources/digitized/.
- Table 12 gives 29 suggested total Cl2 ionization values from 11.5 to 100 eV.
  The suggested curve averages two experimental datasets even though their
  magnitudes differ by more than their combined quoted uncertainties.
- No partial electron-impact ionization measurements exist, so the relative
  production of Cl2+ and Cl+ is unknown.
- The printed thresholds are 11.481 eV for ground-state Cl2 ionization,
  15.45 eV for dissociative ionization, and 11.9 +/- 0.2 eV for ion-pair
  formation.
