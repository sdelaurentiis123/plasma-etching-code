"To construct the gas phase reaction mechanism for CHF3 plasma etching, the
38 reactions in Table 9 were added to the 122 reactions in Table 2 that do not
involve the C2F6 itself."

"For electron-impact reactions of CHF3, the rate parameters are primarily
based on a cross section set recently published by Kushner and Zhang [53]."

"There are also a few estimated rate parameters. Electron impact reactions
for H and H2 were taken from Janev, et al. [54] The remaining ion-ion reactions
or dissociative recombination reactions have estimated rate parameters."
