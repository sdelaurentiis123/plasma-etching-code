Printed values and SI conversion
--------------------------------
The 1995 transport table prints the density-reduced mobilities

  N mu_Cl2+ = 5.62e19 cm^-1 V^-1 s^-1
  N mu_Cl+  = 6.48e19 cm^-1 V^-1 s^-1
  N mu_Cl-  = 6.48e19 cm^-1 V^-1 s^-1.

Since 1 cm^-1 = 100 m^-1, the exact SI values are

  N mu_Cl2+ = 5.62e21 m^-1 V^-1 s^-1
  N mu_Cl+  = 6.48e21 m^-1 V^-1 s^-1
  N mu_Cl-  = 6.48e21 m^-1 V^-1 s^-1.

The 1995 paper assumes a constant ion temperature of 0.12 eV. It states that
ion mobilities and diffusivities were calculated from constant collision
cross sections obtained from ref. 12. Reference 12 is “M. J. Kushner, private
communication”; the acknowledgment likewise thanks Kushner for sharing ion
and neutral collision cross sections. The cross sections and uncertainty are
not printed.

The 2002 paper reprints exactly the same three reduced mobilities and cites
the 1995 source, while Table II assumes gas and ion temperatures of 300 K
(about 0.025852 eV). It does not publish a temperature law or new measurement.

Conflict and executable boundary
--------------------------------
The repository keeps separate exact 1995 and 2002 mobility providers, each
strictly scoped to its printed ion temperature. Both are `published_model`
evidence with no uncertainty and therefore fail predictive grading.

The charged-wall transport provider uses only the positive-ion values. It
evaluates mobility at the reactor neutral density, derives collision frequency
from mu = e/(m nu), computes the ion-speed moment and momentum mean free path,
and passes those values into the Lee–Lieberman electronegative edge closure.
The unused Cl- mobility is retained in the source table but is not silently
treated as sheath-edge transport because the source model confines negative
ions to the plasma bulk.

Predictive closure requires public differential or momentum-transfer data, or
direct swarm mobility measurements, with gas-composition, ion-temperature,
and uncertainty coverage.
