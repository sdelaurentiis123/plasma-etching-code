VERIFIED EXTRACT — report section B.3, CF2 loss in the afterglow

The report fits the afterglow CF2 decay with first- and second-order loss
terms.  Under the stated residence-time assumption, the average CF2 wall-loss
rate is 1.0 +/- 0.8 s^-1.  The corresponding CF2 sticking coefficient in the
afterglow is (8 +/- 6) x 10^-3 at 298 K.

CLAIM BOUNDARY
- This is a directly inferred afterglow wall-loss coefficient with a large
  uncertainty, not an ion-activated wafer sticking coefficient.
- It supports the low end of the independently reported CF2 sticking range;
  it does not identify C4F6 or C4F8 parent sticking.
