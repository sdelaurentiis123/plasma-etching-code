Neufeld, Janzen, and Aziz, Journal of Chemical Physics 57, 1100--1102
(1972), DOI 10.1063/1.1678363.

Verified implementation extract
================================

The original article computes 16 reduced Lennard--Jones (12-6) transport
collision integrals over 0.3 <= T* <= 100. Its Eq. 2 has the form

Omega(l,s)* = A/T*^B + C/exp(D T*) + E/exp(F T*)
              + G/exp(H T*) + R T*^B sin(S T*^W - P).

For the Table-I (1,1) row used by binary diffusion, R, S, W, and P are blank,
and the coefficients are

A = 1.06036     B = 0.15610
C = 0.19300     D = 0.47635
E = 1.03587     F = 1.52996
G = 1.76474     H = 3.89411.

Table II reports average deviation 0.036% and maximum deviation 0.11% at
T* = 0.75 for the (1,1) equation.

Retrieval and audit boundary
============================

The official AIP record confirms the citation, pages, DOI, reduced-temperature
domain, and stated sub-0.1% probable error. The equation and Table-I/II rows
above were checked in text extracted from a scan of the original article at
https://www.scribd.com/document/832627226/Neufeld-Colision-Integral-Formula-ORIGINAL-PAPER .
The AIP title page was visually checked from the hosted scan; the coefficient
table still lacks a locally archived publisher-PDF pixel audit. This boundary
is recorded rather than silently upgraded.

Only the equation, coefficient row, domain, and error row above are consumed
by petch. No coefficient was selected against reactor, wafer, profile, or
feature-depth data.
