The paper says its measured lifetimes agree with Singh et al. (2006) and
Oliver & Hibbert (2013) within 15% when the printed 2-sigma uncertainty is
considered. It attributes larger discrepancies in older calculations to
configuration mixing. It also identifies the 1094.77 A state as particularly
mixed and notes that Oliver & Hibbert changed its designation.

Boundary: these are radiative lifetimes/oscillator strengths, not
electron-impact excitation rates or silicon photoetch yields.
