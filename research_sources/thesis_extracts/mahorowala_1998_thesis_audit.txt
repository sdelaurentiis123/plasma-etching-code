Verified experiment description — thesis Chapter 2, pp. 31-42
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Reactor: Lam TCP 9400SE inductively coupled plasma; separately RF-biased,
  electrostatically clamped 200-mm wafer; 60 degrees C wafer-electrode setting.
- Gas: pure Cl2 or pure HBr; 10 mTorr for all experiments.
- Structures: 500 nm poly-Si, about 200 nm oxide mask or about 475 nm
  photoresist after ARC etch, 250 nm lines, 250 nm to 2.5 um spacings.
- Design variables: 250-550 W inductive power, 20-140 W RF bias, and
  25-175 sccm feed flow.
- Table 2.1 (photoresist/Cl2) and Table 2.3 (photoresist/HBr) vary time to
  approximately equalize depth. They are not blind absolute-depth boards.
- Table 2.2 (oxide-mask/Cl2) fixes every run at 75 s. Eleven of thirteen runs
  report poly-Si and oxide etch rates; runs 8 and 12 are marked overetched.
  The reported poly-Si range is 900-3675 Angstrom/min, dimensionally
  equivalent to 112.5-459.375 nm in 75 s.
- Figure 2.4 maps all thirteen Table-2.2 run numbers to SEM cross-sections of
  250 nm lines and 310 nm spaces.

Verified feature observations — thesis Chapter 2, pp. 42-51
------------------------------------------------------------
- Oxide-masked Cl2 profiles exhibit little visible sidewall deposition and
  minimal mask erosion relative to photoresist-masked Cl2 profiles.
- The source reports early microtrenching in the oxide-mask center-condition
  evolution and attributes the initial trenching to scattering from the
  inclined mask sidewalls.
- A low-bias, high-flow condition develops inward bowing and merging bottom
  trenches, giving morphology targets beyond center depth.

Verified boundary and simulator assumptions — thesis Chapter 4, pp. 106-112
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The thesis estimates, rather than measures per run, about 2 mA/cm2 ion
  current at the wafer, a sheath energy gain near 100 eV, about 120 eV total
  ion energy after adding plasma potential, and neutral/ion ratio near 100.
- Its profile simulations use a reduced neutral/ion ratio of 10, monoenergetic
  35 eV ions, and a 10-degree Gaussian angular FWHM.
- These quantities are model assumptions/estimates, not a measured,
  species-resolved flux plus joint ion energy-angle distribution.

Claim boundary
--------------
Table 2.2 plus Figure 2.4 is an absolute fixed-time chlorine rate/profile
corpus. It can grade an independently validated reactor-boundary provider or
a facility-conditioned profile transfer. It cannot by itself grant a
first-principles knobs-to-depth pass: species-resolved wafer fluxes and
measured per-run IEAD/IAD are not published. The Table-2.2 target rate may not
be used to select a boundary and then be counted as its own prediction.
