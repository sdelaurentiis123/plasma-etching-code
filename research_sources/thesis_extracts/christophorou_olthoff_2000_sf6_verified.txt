Focused verification notes (manual visual audit of the source PDF at 400 dpi):

- Table 16 lists partial positive-ion cross sections at 100 eV from three
  experiments. The executable transcription uses the preferred case-A
  analysis in Ref. 158. Its nine branches sum to 6.51 x 10^-20 m2; Table 17's
  independently recommended total at 100 eV is 6.53 x 10^-20 m2.
- The article warns that the analysis procedures differ by 7-40% for larger
  fragments and by as much as a factor of three for F+.
- Table 24 is the recommended SF6- attachment curve from 0.0001 to 0.40 eV.
- Table 25 is the suggested SF5- dissociative-attachment curve.
- Table 27 gives suggested SF4-, SF3-, SF2-, F2-, and F- curves and explicitly
  uses 10^-22 m2 units. Blank cells are blank in the source.
- The surrounding text says total attachment is represented by the sum of all
  recommended/suggested partial cross sections. It says SF5- dominates below
  about 1.5 eV, F- above that energy, and total attachment is dissociative
  beyond about 0.3 eV.
- Table 28 tabulates the resulting room-temperature total attachment curve.
- Table 3 supplies multiple reported appearance/formation energies. The
  executable closure uses the earliest listed direct-formation threshold for
  each Table-16 branch and preserves that choice in its derivation receipt.
- Figure 15 plots energy-dependent partial positive-ion data, but those curves
  are not tabulated in the review. No curve values are claimed from Table 16.
