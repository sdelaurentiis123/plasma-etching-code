Verified source facts (13-page CC-BY publisher PDF read in full):

1. In a nonconservative electron swarm, flux and bulk transport coefficients
   are distinct kinetic quantities. Time-of-flight measurements generally
   sample universal bulk coefficients.
2. The older Brambring representation used for many pulsed-Townsend analyses
   is not the physical continuity equation when ionization or attachment is
   active. Its fitted transport properties carry tildes and are not themselves
   universal flux or bulk coefficients.
3. Equations 30-32 give the exact reported transformation at the retained
   order:

       R_net = alpha_tilde_T * W_tilde
       W_B   = W_tilde + alpha_tilde_T * D_tilde_L
       D_B,L = D_tilde_L

4. The paper applies this conversion only when W_tilde, alpha_tilde_T, and
   D_tilde_L are available from the same pulsed-Townsend study. Reanalysis of
   the original current transient with the physical equation is preferred.
5. The paper separately notes that a calibrated PT current amplitude can, in
   principle, measure flux drift. That does not turn a transit-time W_tilde
   value into flux drift.

Executable consequence:

Lan-Jeon reports legacy PT Wv but not same-study alpha_tilde_T and D_tilde_L.
Neither a flux-drift calculation nor a modern density-gradient bulk result is
the measurement-equivalent target. Cross sections must not be retuned solely
to force either quantity through Wv. This definition result does not establish
a reactor state, wafer boundary, or feature depth.
