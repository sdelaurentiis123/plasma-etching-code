SOURCE: K.-R. Choi, J.-C. Woo, Y.-H. Joo, Y.-S. Chun, and C.-I. Kim,
"Dry etching properties of TiO2 thin films in O2/CF4/Ar plasma," Vacuum 92,
85-89 (2013), DOI 10.1016/j.vacuum.2012.11.009.

RETRIEVAL: Full article text and figures were read through the publicly
accessible publisher-manuscript relay linked from the DOI record. The raw PDF
could not be archived locally because the relay rejected non-browser download
requests. Values below are source-reported endpoint values in the article text,
not visual digitizations. The source reports no measurement uncertainties for
these plotted responses.

FIXED BASE CONDITION: planar ICP; approximately 200 nm electron-beam-
evaporated TiO2; O2/CF4/Ar = 3/16/4 sccm; 700 W source power; -150 V DC bias;
2 Pa, except for the independently swept variable.

SOURCE-REPORTED RESPONSE ENDPOINTS:
- Oxygen sweep, fixed CF4/Ar = 16/4 sccm: TiO2 rate is 154.1, 179.4, and
  137.5 nm/min at 0, 3, and 9 sccm O2. TiO2:SiO2 selectivity changes from
  0.57 to 0.81 over the reported 0-to-9-sccm interval.
- Source-power sweep, 600 to 800 W: TiO2 rate changes from 136.0 to
  208.3 nm/min and TiO2:SiO2 selectivity from 0.66 to 0.83.
- DC-bias-magnitude sweep, 50 to 250 V: TiO2 rate changes from 130.9 to
  197.2 nm/min and TiO2:SiO2 selectivity from 0.65 to 0.56.
- Pressure sweep, 1.2 to 2.8 Pa: TiO2 rate changes from 187.7 to
  138.7 nm/min and TiO2:SiO2 selectivity from 0.60 to 0.50.
- AFM RMS roughness: 36.5 angstrom as deposited, 59.8 angstrom after CF4/Ar,
  and 29.8 angstrom after O2/CF4/Ar exposure.

SOURCE INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY:
- XPS supports Ti-F bond formation after fluorocarbon-plasma exposure.
- The article attributes the bias response to ion-assisted bond breaking and
  reaction-product desorption.
- The oxygen response is nonmonotonic; excess oxygen is described as blocking
  fluorine adsorption.
- No ion flux, species-resolved IEAD, radical flux, or uncertainty is reported.
  Consequently these data constrain response signs and required state topology
  but do not identify a microscopic yield or a coefficient transferable to the
  Oxford NPG80 condition.
