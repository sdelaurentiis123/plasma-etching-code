Tinacba et al. 2021 SF5+ beam/MD audit extract
================================================

Source
------
E. J. C. Tinacba, T. Ito, K. Karahashi, M. Isobe, and S. Hamaguchi,
"Molecular dynamics simulation for reactive ion etching of Si and SiO2 by
SF5+ ions," Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology B 39, 043203 (2021).
DOI: 10.1116/6.0001230

Audited source locations
------------------------
Printed page 043203-2:
- SF5+ was mass selected and decelerated to 150--2000 eV.
- "The mass and energy of incident ions were measured by an energy-mass
  analyzer"
- Beam current was measured by a Faraday cup at the sample position.
- No radical beam was used.
- Depth was measured with a Dektak3ST contact profilometer.
- The experimental conversion is Y = d*N/D.

Printed pages 043203-2--6:
- Si/O/F use previously published modified Stillinger-Weber potentials.
- The S-F carrier potential was conditioned on B3LYP/6-311G DFT plus a
  published S-F bond energy/distance.
- S-S, S-Si, and S-O chemistry was intentionally suppressed; sulfur retains
  carrier bonding to F plus its physical mass/radius.
- A typical trajectory uses 4000 successive impacts on one evolving surface.

Printed page 043203-7, Figure 8:
- DFT-informed MD open diamonds are compared with independent experimental
  filled circles for Si and SiO2 at 150 and 2000 eV.
- Figure render and marker centers are audited in
  data/experimental/tinacba_2021/.

Printed page 043203-8, Figure 10:
- At 2000 eV, the MD depth slopes are 12.5 nm (Si) and 13.6 nm (SiO2) per
  1e16 cm^-2.

Claim boundary
--------------
This source validates a mass-selected, normal-incidence SF5+ atomistic surface
provider. It does not supply an SF6 reactor boundary, an ion-mixture law,
off-normal response, neutral-F synergy, or general sulfur-surface chemistry.
The full article was read for this audit; the copyrighted PDF is checksum
pinned but not redistributed in the repository.
