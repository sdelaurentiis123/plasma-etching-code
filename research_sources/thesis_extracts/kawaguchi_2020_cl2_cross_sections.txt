Cl2 gas and Cl2/N2 mixtures

     A detailed and reliable electron collision cross section set of Cl2 gas is constructed. The present cross section set consists of one elastic
     momentum transfer, three vibrational excitation, eight dissociative electronic excitation, two electronic excitation, one ion-pair formation, one
     dissociative electron attachment, and four ionization cross sections. The electron drift velocity, longitudinal diffusion coefficient, ionization
     coefficient, and electron attachment coefficient in both Cl2 gas and Cl2/N2 mixtures and the electron attachment rate coefficient in mixtures of N2
     gas and a minute amount of Cl2 gas are calculated by Monte Carlo simulation using the present cross section set, and then the reliability of the
     present cross section set is demonstrated by comparing calculated and measured electron transport coefficients and rate coefficients.
     Furthermore, the rate coefficients for both elastic and inelastic electron collisions and the mean energy of electrons in Cl2 gas are calculated
     from the present cross section set and are provided. © 2020 The Japan Society of Applied Physics
