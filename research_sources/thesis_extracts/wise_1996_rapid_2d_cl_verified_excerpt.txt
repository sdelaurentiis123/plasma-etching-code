Architecture (pp. 2499--2500):
- Electromagnetic power deposition feeds an electron-energy module.
- Electron-impact rates feed separate neutral and charged transport modules.
- Modules are iterated to a self-consistent solution by equation splitting.
- The bulk and sheath are separated to avoid resolving the thin sheath inside
  the bulk finite-element solve.

Bulk charged closure (p. 2500):
- Quasineutrality is imposed in the bulk.
- Positive- and negative-ion particle balances are solved.
- Electron density follows from electroneutrality.
- Boltzmann electron equilibrium closes the electric field through the
  electron-pressure gradient.

Wall closure (p. 2500):
- Positive-ion wall flux is local ion density times local Bohm velocity.
- Negative-ion density is zero at the wall.
- The sheath is a potential discontinuity outside the bulk model.

Base case and spatial source (p. 2500):
- 20 mTorr, 180 W plasma power, 20 sccm pure chlorine, 13.56 MHz.
- No substrate bias, wafer, or etching.
- The calculated power deposition is toroidal and decays over about 1 cm
  beneath the coil.

Validation boundary (pp. 2500--2501):
- Compared line-integrated electron density, central negative-ion density,
  and radial electron density, electron temperature, and plasma potential
  with GEC-ICP measurements.
- The authors report no adjusted reaction rates for the comparison.
- Negative-ion measurement uncertainty is stated to be about a factor of two.
- Predicted electron temperature is about 50 percent below the probe result.
