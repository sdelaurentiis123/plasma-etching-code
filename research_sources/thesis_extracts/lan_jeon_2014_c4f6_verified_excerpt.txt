Verified source facts (publisher PDF visually checked at 600 dpi):

1. Tables 1 and 2 print the momentum-transfer and nine inelastic C4F6
   collision curves in units of 1e-16 cm2. The committed SI tables preserve
   every printed numeric row and convert with 1e-20 m2 per source unit.
2. The authors constructed much of the set by trial-and-error adjustment of
   cross sections until a two-term Boltzmann calculation reproduced measured
   pulsed-Townsend drift in pure C4F6 and C4F6/Ar mixtures. It is therefore a
   swarm-inverted working set, not ten independent differential measurements.
3. The stated fit domain is 0.35-1200 Td. The authors call agreement within
   +/-5% acceptable.
4. The drift observable is explicitly described as average electron drift
   velocity Wv obtained by the pulsed-Townsend method.
5. Figure 7 contains the pure-C4F6 measured markers used by the executable
   board. The archived seven-page PDF contains Figures 1-7 even though the
   text later refers to Figure 8.

Evidence boundary:

- These tables constrain local electron collision kinetics within the stated
  swarm inversion and two-term limits.
- They do not identify a reactor state, absorbed power, ion composition,
  wafer flux, surface yield, or feature depth.
- The legacy PT quantity Wv must not be silently relabeled as flux or modern
  bulk drift. See casey-2021-pt-foundations for the definition conversion.
