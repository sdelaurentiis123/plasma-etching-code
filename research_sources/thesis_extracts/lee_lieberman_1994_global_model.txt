Geometry and assumptions (report p.3; PDF p.7)
------------------------------------------------
"The cylindrical reactor geometry chosen was based on that of a commercially
available TCP (transformer coupled plasma) reactor, with L = 7.5 cm and
R = 15.25 cm."

All densities are volume averaged. The neutral temperature is 600 K. The ion
temperature is 0.5 eV below 1 mTorr and approaches the 600 K thermal
temperature at higher pressure.

Power and particle balances (report pp.4--8; PDF pp.8--12)
-----------------------------------------------------------
P_abs = P_ev + P_iw + P_ew.

The electron volume-loss term sums reaction frequency multiplied by threshold
energy, including ionization, excitation, and elastic loss. The electron wall
energy is epsilon_ew = 2 Te. The text states that ion wall energy epsilon_iw is
typically 5--8 Te for high-density sources.

For positive-ion species i:

V n_e nu_iz,i =
    A n_is u_B,i
    + V k_rec,i n_i n_-
    + V sum_j k_cx,ij n_i n_j.

Quasineutrality at the sheath edge is n_es = sum_i n_is.

The effective ion-loss area is:

A_eff = h_L (2 pi R^2) + h_R (2 pi R L).

For an electropositive discharge (alpha_avg = 0), Eqs. 13--14 reduce to:

h_L = 0.86 /
      sqrt(3 + L/(2 lambda)
             + (0.86 L u_B / (pi D_a))^2)

h_R = 0.8 /
      sqrt(4 + R/lambda
             + (0.8 R u_B /
                (2.405 J_1(2.405) D_a))^2)

The neutral diffusion length is:

1/Lambda^2 = (pi/L)^2 + (2.405/R)^2.

Argon reaction set (Table 3; report p.25; PDF p.29)
----------------------------------------------------
Te is in eV. Printed coefficients are in cm^3 s^-1 unless stated otherwise.

1. e + Ar -> Ar+ + 2e
   k1 = 1.23e-7 exp(-18.68/Te)

2. e + Ar -> Ar* + e
   k2 = 3.71e-8 exp(-15.06/Te)

3. e + Ar* -> Ar+ + 2e
   k3 = 2.05e-7 exp(-4.95/Te)

4. e + Ar* -> Ar + e
   k4 = 2.0e-7

5. Ar* + Ar* -> Ar + Ar+
   k5 = 6.2e-10

6. Ar+ -> Ar at the wall
   k6 = 2 u_B,Ar+ (R^2 h_L + R L h_R)/(R^2 L) s^-1

7. Ar* -> Ar at the wall
   k7 = D_eff/Lambda^2 s^-1

Conservation note
-----------------
The printed fifth reaction is customary associative-ionization shorthand. If
read literally it violates charge conservation; the emitted electron must be
included in a closed reaction ledger:

Ar* + Ar* -> Ar + Ar+ + e.

Likewise, the printed ion wall-loss row is not a closed gas-phase elementary
reaction. A current-balanced closed wall-return event consumes the matching
wall electron, while an open plasma balance must carry ion and electron wall
losses as explicit boundary exchanges.

Published comparison conditions (report p.9 and caption p.19)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Unless otherwise indicated: absorbed power 1000 W and total flow 35 SCCM.
Figure 3 plots Te versus pressure for Ar, O2, and Cl2 with absorbed power
1000 W, Q = 35 SCCM, and gamma_rec = 0.

Figure 8 combines three separate argon experiments (Ra, Mahoney, Oomori).
The report states that each model curve uses the corresponding operating
conditions and reactor geometry. Those primary conditions must be recovered
before a quantitative independent-validation claim.
