Abstract
We have measured the electron drift velocity, density-normalized longitudinal diffusion and
effective ionization coefﬁcients for electrons in Cl2 and Cl2–N2 mixtures over the combined E/N
range from 3 to 450 Td, using a pulsed Townsend apparatus especially designed to withstand the
action of corrosive gases. Our measurements for the electron drift velocity and the effective
ionization coefﬁcient are compared with the only two previous measurements available, one of
them indirect. In view of the present incompleteness and uncertainty in the electron collision
cross section set for Cl2, Cl2–N2 mixtures with Cl2 contents from 1% to 80% have been
measured in the aim of shedding more light into the completeness and reliability of the cross
section set by testing the cross sections derived for pure Cl2 with those of the Cl2–N2 mixture for
which the cross sections for N2 are well known.

Keywords: chlorine, Cl2, electron transport, drift velocity, electron attachment, electron swarm
coefﬁcients, longitudinal diffusion coefﬁcient

     Thirty-two years later, in 1967, Božin and Goodyear [11]
used a steady-state swarm experiment to measure the density-
normalized ionization, α/N, and electron attachment, η/N,
coefﬁcients in pure Cl2 over the E/N range 210–450 Td
(Townsend=10−16 V cm2), and calculated the density-
normalized effective ionization coefﬁcient, (α−η)/N, up to
750 Td. The pressure range used was 1–10 Torr (1 Torr=
133.3 Pa). No particular dependence of these coefﬁcients with
pressure was found over the entire E/N range covered.
     It appears from rather recent compilations that other
important data of electron interactions with Cl2 such as the
momentum transfer cross sections have never been reported
[12]; instead, their values have been calculated from the
above mentioned swarm data using Boltzmann methods
[13, 14]. On the one hand, these cross sections deserve
revision and, on the other, newer and more reliable mea-
surements are needed, as stated in [15].
     This research is aimed at providing, for the ﬁrst time, a
consistent set of electron swarm coefﬁcients in Cl2 and its
mixtures with N2, namely W, (α−η)/N and the density-nor-
malized longitudinal diffusion coefﬁcient, NDL. A special
apparatus has been designed for these measurements. The
Cl2–N2 mixture was studied with the purpose of validating
and/or deriving cross sections provided that the cross section
set for N2 is already well established.
