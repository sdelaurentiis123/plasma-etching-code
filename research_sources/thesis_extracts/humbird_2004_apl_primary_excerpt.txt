VERIFIED CLAIMS
- Reactive MD injects thermal CF2 and normally incident Ar+ at a 9:1 ratio
  onto Si, comparing 20 and 200 eV ions in a 470 Å^2 cell.
- At 20 eV, below the model's physical sputter threshold, ions dissociate CF2
  and create CF and SiFx while the film F/C remains near two.
- At 200 eV, the model produces a deeper Si-C-F region and appreciable Si
  etching.  Pure-Si physical sputtering is only 0.11 Si/ion at that energy;
  fluorochemical enhancement is therefore required.
- The simulated Si-C-F region grows from roughly 15 Å at 20 eV to roughly
  30 Å at 200 eV.
- Across 20 to 200 eV, destroyed C-F bonds and newly formed Si-F bonds show
  nearly one-to-one correspondence.

CLAIM BOUNDARY
The one-to-one bond conversion and mixing depths are mechanistic MD
constraints under the simulated CF2/Ar+ boundary.  They are not measured
wafer fluxes and do not by themselves identify a reactor boundary.
