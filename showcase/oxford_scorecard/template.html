Net: the two mechanism claims that were genuinely falsifiable — mask-exhaustion-driven loss with spatial zoning, and no rinse collapse — both hold. The magnitude claim (exhaustion in every block) was too pessimistic: your process sits inside our predicted interval, mostly on the surviving side, which is exactly what a knife-edge looks like when the mask is a few nm thicker than our witness rates assumed.

4 · What would close the remaining rows

  1. One cross-section (cleave + tilt, or FIB) through any intact block → trench depth, pillar height and sidewall angle. Our tilt-geometry estimate (0.25–0.55 µm pillars) has ±150 nm slack because the near sidewall's brightness is ambiguous in InLens; a cross-section removes it.
  2. Pre-etch CD per dose (post-lift-off Cr squares) or the dose→CD calibration → strict CD-transfer scoring.
  3. Which block is which dose value, and the lift-off Cr thickness if it varied — this is the leading candidate for why blocks 1/2/4/5/6 survived while 8/9 did not.
  4. Achieved DC self-bias and, if a witness piece exists, blanket TiO₂ loss → narrows the etch-rate interval that sets the knife-edge.
  5. The rinse/dry step you used — closes the mechanics story completely.
