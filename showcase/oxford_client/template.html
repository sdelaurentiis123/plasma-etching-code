What this means

The physics is right: the simulation identified the failure mechanism of your process — the mask, not the pillars — and the geometry of what survives, from the recipe alone. Where it was off, it was off in a known direction: the Cr etch rate we had to borrow from other labs' tools is a few nm/min too fast for yours, which is exactly the difference between five blocks surviving and none. That is a proof of concept that works.
