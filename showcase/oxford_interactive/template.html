What this means

We said the Cr cap would run out during the etch and the pillar tops would be attacked — as zones, because the process sits on a knife-edge — and that the pillars would not fall over at rinse. Your frames show exactly that: zoned loss to stubs, flat-topped survivors 0.25–0.55 µm tall, zero toppled pillars. We were too pessimistic on how many blocks fail (we said nine, you lost four) because the Cr etch rate we borrowed from other labs' tools is a few nm/min faster than yours. One cross-section and your DC self-bias reading close the last two numbers.
